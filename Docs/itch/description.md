## Description (วางในช่อง Description)

**Island Rocket Rumble** is a cute 2-player rocket battle on one keyboard.
Pip and Momo live on two floating islands. Between them sits **cpe40**, a cross-eyed bulldozer robot that hates being bothered.
Collect falling parts, **engineer** your own rockets piece by piece on your launch pad, and be the first to sink your friend's island!

### Inspired by CPE102 Engineering Exploration
Like an engineering project, nothing comes for free: gather the right materials, follow the recipe, build, test, and upgrade.
Every part you pick up shows up on your launch pad as a real rocket piece — you watch your design come together.

### How to play
- **Collect parts** that drop on your island. Every 45 seconds the tier goes up (Scrap → Copper → Silver → Gold → Crystal). Old parts convert into the new tier (2 old = 1 new).
- **Build & launch**: walk into your craft ring, pick a rocket (T1–T5) and fire it at your opponent's island — or at the robot.
- **Shoot the robot** to make it drop extra parts. Hit its glowing **weak point** for double drops. But the angrier it gets, the more it transforms and throws gems that stun you!
- **Repair** your island, or spend parts on **upgrade cards** (attack, defense, mobility — pick 1 of 3).
- **Power-ups** appear on your island: shield, speed shoes, magnet and heart.
- Bigger rockets need longer to recharge. Island HP 0 = **K.O.** … and a **FATALITY!**
- Time up? Higher HP wins. Tied? **Sudden death** — first hit wins.

### Controls
| | Player 1 (Pip) | Player 2 (Momo) |
|---|---|---|
| Move / menu | W A S D | Arrow keys |
| Craft + fire / confirm | SPACE | ENTER |
| Settings / pause | ESC or the gear button | |

In the craft menu: left/right = choose rocket, repair or upgrade · up/down = switch target (enemy island / robot).

### Features
- Rockets that build up piece by piece as you collect parts
- Robot boss with 4 forms and a weak point
- 12 upgrade cards, 4 power-ups, island repair
- Character customization: hats, colors, face and back accessories
- Settings: volume, fullscreen, screen shake, match length, island HP
- All 3D models, sounds and music are generated by code inside Unity — no third-party assets

## Download & install instructions (วางในช่องนี้)
1. Download `IslandRocketRumble_Windows.zip`
2. Right-click → **Extract All** (do not run it from inside the zip)
3. Open `IslandRocketRumble.exe`
4. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway** (the game is not code-signed).

Windows 64-bit. No install needed.
